\section{Basic Table with Booktabs}

The \texttt{booktabs} package provides \verb|\toprule|, \verb|\midrule|,
and \verb|\bottomrule| for clean, professional tables.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Element} & \textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Atomic \#} \\
    \midrule
    Hydrogen  & H  & 1  \\
    Helium    & He & 2  \\
    Lithium   & Li & 3  \\
    Carbon    & C  & 6  \\
    Nitrogen  & N  & 7  \\
    Oxygen    & O  & 8  \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Selected elements from the periodic table.}
\label{tab:elements}
\end{table}

\section{Multicolumn and Multirow}

Use \verb|\multicolumn{n}{align}{text}| to span columns
and \verb|\multirow{n}{width}{text}| to span rows.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{llcc}
    \toprule
    \multicolumn{2}{c}{\textbf{Student Info}} & \textbf{Math} & \textbf{Science} \\
    \cmidrule(lr){1-2} \cmidrule(lr){3-3} \cmidrule(lr){4-4}
    \textbf{Name} & \textbf{ID} & & \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{2}{*}{Alice}  & 001 & 95 & 88 \\
                             & 002 & 78 & 92 \\
    \midrule
    \multirow{2}{*}{Bob}    & 003 & 82 & 75 \\
                             & 004 & 90 & 85 \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Grades with merged name cells.}
\label{tab:grades}
\end{table}

\section{Colored Rows}

Alternating row colors improve readability in large tables.

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\rowcolors{2}{gray!15}{white}  % alternate starting from row 2
\begin{tabular}{lll}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Day} & \textbf{High (\textdegree C)} & \textbf{Low (\textdegree C)} \\
    \midrule
    Monday    & 22 & 14 \\
    Tuesday   & 25 & 16 \\
    Wednesday & 19 & 12 \\
    Thursday  & 21 & 13 \\
    Friday    & 27 & 18 \\
    Saturday  & 30 & 20 \\
    Sunday    & 28 & 19 \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Weekly temperature forecast.}
\label{tab:weather}
\end{table}

\section{Custom Column Types}

The \texttt{array} package lets you define reusable column types.

\newcolumntype{L}[1]{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{#1}}
\newcolumntype{C}[1]{>{\centering\arraybackslash}p{#1}}
\newcolumntype{R}[1]{>{\raggedleft\arraybackslash}p{#1}}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{L{4cm} C{3cm} R{3cm}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Description} & \textbf{Quantity} & \textbf{Price (\$)} \\
    \midrule
    Widget (standard model, blue)  & 150  & 4.99  \\
    Gadget (premium, with case)    & 75   & 12.50 \\
    Thingamajig (bulk pack of 10)  & 200  & 1.25  \\
    \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Inventory with fixed-width columns.}
\label{tab:inventory}
\end{table}

See Table~\ref{tab:elements} for elements, Table~\ref{tab:grades} for grades,
Table~\ref{tab:weather} for weather, and Table~\ref{tab:inventory} for inventory.
